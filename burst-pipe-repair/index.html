A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Tallahassee Restoration Co responds to burst pipe emergencies across Tallahassee and the nearby communities. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • A musty smell near a wall, cabinet or floor with plumbing behind it
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast

Why Burst Pipe Repair Is Not a DIY Job

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.
