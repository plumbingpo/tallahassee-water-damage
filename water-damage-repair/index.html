Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.

How to Tell You Need Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.
  • Paint is bubbling, peeling or stained brown where water ran down the wall.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • The subfloor gives or feels spongy underfoot where water once stood.
  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.

Our Water Damage Repair Process

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

We take final moisture readings on framing, subfloor and remaining drywall. Reconstruction only begins once those numbers are at normal levels for the material, so nothing damp gets sealed in.

2

Put the Scope in Writing

We hand you a list, room by room, covering what gets replaced, planned materials and the likely schedule. When there is an open claim, the scope is coordinated with your adjuster.

3

Reconstruct and Install

Insulation goes back into open cavities, drywall is hung, taped and finished, and subfloor is repaired. After that we install trim, cabinetry and floors, ordering the work so completed surfaces are not damaged.

4

Finish and Walk Through

Texture is matched, walls are primed and painted, and baseboards, doors and hardware go back on. Before calling it done, our team tours all the rooms with you and takes care of each item noted.
