A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Tallahassee Restoration Co handles emergency flood cleanup across Tallahassee and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
